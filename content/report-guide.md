## Evidence for the separate reports and presentation

Milestone 4 evaluates the product. The group report, individual report, peer review and presentation are separate submissions with their own criteria. This site supplies evidence for them; it does not replace the required analysis or claim those submissions are complete.

The [current official brief](documents/coms3011a-2026-official-brief.pdf#page=12) asks for reasoned analysis: explain the decision, its motivation, what happened, and what the team learned. Cite a small selection of dated artifacts in an appendix instead of importing the whole site.

## Group report — 130 marks

| Required section | Marks | Evidence and analysis starting point |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Introduction | 10 | [Purpose and scope](#site-guide) · [Requirements](#requirements): explain the product and the report's argument. |
| Methodology and planning | 30 | [Team process](#process), [roadmaps](#roadmap), [stakeholder decisions](#stakeholder-decisions): evaluate how task choice, scope and sequential integration evolved. |
| Architecture | 20 | [Architecture](#architecture), [API](#api), [database](#database), [third-party register](#third-party-code): assess technology choices, onboarding and trade-offs. |
| Testing | 20 | [Testing](#testing), [user feedback](#user-feedback), [bug tracker](#bug-tracker): explain how the strategy changed and which feedback produced useful fixes. |
| Deployment and integration | 20 | [Deployment](#deployment), [Git method](#git-methodology), [holiday adapter](#integration): evaluate CI, hosting, mirror problems and external-service usefulness. |
| General discussion | 20 | [Release history](#sprint4), [known limitations](#milestone4/known-limitations): discuss recurring challenges and future improvements. |
| Conclusion | 5 | Synthesise the strongest findings from the analysis. |
| Supporting documents / appendix | 5 | Link selected commits, issue records, diagrams and dated evidence from [Sources](#references). |

## Individual report — stated total 100 marks

| Required section | Printed marks | Evidence to select for the author's own contribution |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Introduction | 10 | Personal role and the scope of the reflection. |
| Responsibilities and contributions | 20 | [Member work records](#work-tracker), own branches/PRs and delivered user stories. |
| Technical challenges and solutions | 20 | Relevant [bug records](#bug-tracker), failed/successful CI evidence and design trade-offs. |
| Learning and development | 20 | Technologies learned, AI use and its effects; [AI declaration](#references/ai-declaration) and [technology register](#third-party-code). |
| Collaboration and teamwork | 10 | [Task choice and voting](#process), reviews, integration and communication examples. |
| General discussion | 10 | Work the author is proud of, would redo, and the reasons. |
| Conclusion | 10 | Personal learning and what would change next time. |
| Supporting documents / appendix | 5 | Selected evidence attributable to the author. |

**Brief arithmetic:** section 3.6 prints these eight weights, which add to 105, while its Total row states 100. This guide preserves the published figures rather than silently changing a weighting. The lecturer's interpretation governs marking.

## Peer review

Section 3.7 requires a **200–300 word paragraph for each group member**, a rating from **0 to 5**, and a self-rating. Describe observed contributions with specific examples. The personal evaluation and anonymous peer review are submitted separately; no peer ratings are invented or published here. See the [official instructions](documents/coms3011a-2026-official-brief.pdf#page=14).

## Group presentation — 110 marks

| Criterion | Marks | Useful material |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Product showcase | 40 | Product overview, selected real role journeys, interface and recovery states. |
| Technical breakdown | 20 | [Architecture](#architecture), [diagrams](#diagrams), one complete UI → API → database flow. |
| Presentation quality | 40 | Clear structure, timing, project coverage, visual aids and shared delivery. |
| Questions | 10 | Explain choices, limits and code behaviour; involve the relevant team members. |

A demonstration should distinguish a design mockup, recorded evidence and a working live feature. [Official presentation rubric](documents/coms3011a-2026-official-brief.pdf#page=15).
